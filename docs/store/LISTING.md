## Description
VetTV Direct Stream turns Xbox Game Bar into a one-click broadcast
studio for streamers who would rather play than configure.

**One-click Go Live.** Open Game Bar (Win+G), choose a platform,
quality and encoder, and start streaming. Presets cover Twitch,
YouTube, Facebook, Kick, Rumble and VetTV ingest; anything else works
through a custom RTMP URL with stream-key placeholder support.

**Real hardware encoding.** The bundled encoder auto-detects NVIDIA
NVENC, Intel Quick Sync and AMD AMF, and falls back to software x264 —
720p, 1080p and 1440p at 30 or 60 fps with the 2-second keyframe
interval platforms require.

**Game + microphone audio.** Desktop/game audio and your microphone
are mixed into a single AAC track, with per-source volume sliders so
you can balance game sound against your voice. Your stream key is kept in the
Windows Credential Manager, never in plaintext, and diagnostic logs
redact keys automatically.

**Chat that floats over your game.** Pin the companion Direct Stream
Chat widget and follow the chat of the platform you are streaming to —
Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Facebook, Rumble, VetTV or a custom chat page —
without alt-tabbing out of your match.

**Private by design.** No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no VetTV
servers: your video, audio and stream key travel directly from your PC
to the platform you chose. See the privacy policy for details.

Requirements: Windows 10 (19041+) or Windows 11, 64-bit, Xbox Game Bar.
A virtual-audio-capturer device is needed for desktop audio capture.
